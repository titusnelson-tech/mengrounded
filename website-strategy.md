# Mengrounded Website Strategy (Final Decisions)

**Purpose:** This file exists so any future agent working on the mengrounded.com project can quickly get up to speed on the direction without needing the full conversation history.

## Core Audience
Average working guy (trades, construction, stagehand, mechanic, etc.) who is quietly searching for help with anger, relationships, guilt, regret, or things from the past. He is not looking for therapy language — he wants straight talk and practical steps.

## Desired Feeling on the Site
When someone lands on the site, they should think: “This is a guy who’s been through hard stuff and actually knows how to help.”

## Tone
Kind, welcoming, honest, and straightforward. No fluff. No therapy-speak.

## Theme / Vibe
Clean, warm, professional, and grounded. 
- Avoid literal wood/forest or “Midwest cabin” aesthetic.
- Use the warm masculine color palette (deep greens, warm browns, charcoal, soft creams) in a modern, steady way.
- The site should feel solid and trustworthy, not themed or overly emotional.

## Personal Story
Use a **balanced summary** (2–4 short paragraphs) on the About page. Enough to show credibility and “I’ve been there,” but not a deep, detailed life story. Focus on the challenges and how he learned to move forward.

## Contact Method
Low-pressure email contact only. No public email address shown in copy. Contact form submits to **Formspree** (endpoint `https://formspree.io/f/xrpgzbyl`), which delivers to **titus.nelson@gmail.com**. Free tier: 50 submissions/month. Reply expectation: **a day or two**. Crisis line under the form: "If you're in crisis, call or text 988."

## Sessions
- Phone calls for now (video calls later, not yet).
- Set aside about **one hour**; sometimes 20 minutes is enough.
- **Free of charge — do NOT post that it's free.** Titus tells people that when they reach out.
- After a conversation, people may **donate to the project** if they want. Donation button on the Contact page links to **https://www.paypal.me/mengrounded**.

## Location
- Target **Los Angeles** for SEO (metadata + footer mention only). Do not make LA a hard requirement in copy — Titus expects to serve people all over as it grows.

## Testimonials
- Current testimonials (Mark R., Derek T., James K.) are **placeholders**. Replace with real ones when they come in. Do not present them as real.

## Words / Phrases to Avoid
healing, journey, trauma, inner child, vulnerability (soft sense), self-care, breakthrough, transformation.

## Writing Rules
- **No em dashes (—) anywhere.** Use periods, commas, or colons instead.
- **No repeated phrases across pages.** Each idea gets one home (e.g. "I've been through my own share of messes" lives only on the About page).
- Homepage stays short and scannable; no card sections that just duplicate the nav menu.

## Main Goal
Get people to reach out (via email). Everything on the site should support trust and make the first step feel safe and low-pressure.

## Other Notes
- Testimonials can stay on their own page for now.
- Keep the homepage relatively short and scannable.
- The site should feel welcoming for men without being overtly “masculine” or bro-style.
